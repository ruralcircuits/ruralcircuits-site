Sage

Nourishing

A regenerative partner AI that lives on your own land—helping you nourish the earth and its inhabitants, building resilience and independence.

  • Garden planning and harvest tracking
  • Recipe guidance from your pantry and land
  • Care support for chickens and farm animals
  • Works offline, respects your privacy

Mycelium

Taking Root

A living network that connects neighbors, sensors, and shared knowledge—like mycelium beneath the forest floor, quietly exchanging what's needed.

  • Share observations and resources with your community
  • Connect soil sensors, weather stations, resources
  • Collective wisdom from the land
  • Distributed, resilient, community-owned
🪨

Whisper Stones

Germinating

Physical nodes you can build yourself—markers on the land that listen, sense, and share. Open hardware for open communities.

  • Solar-powered sensing stations
  • Simple to build, simple to repair
  • Monitor soil, air, water, weather
  • Open designs, local materials

How It All Connects

Sage runs right on your own computer—private, yours, not owned by tech companies burning fossil fuels in billion-dollar data centers. She helps you tend your garden, manage your pantry, and care for your homestead, offering daily guidance informed by sensors gathering real data from your land. Plant this today. Make that dish for dinner. Everything works on your own property, no outside internet needed—unless you want to connect with your rural neighbors through Mycelium to share knowledge, barter resources, and build community. All open source, all designed to serve the land and those who care for it.
